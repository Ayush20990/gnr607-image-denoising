Satellite data

Sentinel-2, Netherlands: contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data (2016), processed by ESA. Near-infrared contributes to the false colour; vegetation appears red. Two 768 × 768 crops from a 9725 × 10223 JPEG; no resizing or artificial upscaling. The exact ground sampling distance of this processed JPEG is not asserted. These are display channels, not calibrated spectral bands. Source, licence and crop coordinates are in data/source_manifest.json.

Method and fair selection

For each original channel with mean μ, set σ = pμ and add independent Uniform(−√3σ,+√3σ) samples. p is 5%, 10%, or 20%. RGB channels are processed independently. KNN selects the k intensities nearest to the noisy centre within an odd spatial window, including the centre. Mean filtering averages every entry. Both read an unchanged noisy image; borders are replicated. No clipping or rounding before MSE.

Candidate windows: 3, 5, 7. Candidate k values: rounded 35%, 55%, 75%, 90% of window area. For each image/noise level, choose the pair minimising the average of KNN MSE and mean MSE on validation seed 60700. Hold the pair fixed for test seeds 60701–60703. This uses clean-reference calibration on the same images; it does not demonstrate generalisation to unseen scenes. Noise streams are paired across levels.

The accelerated NumPy implementation was checked against the original manual algorithm on nine cases, including k=1 and k=window area. The original manual implementation is retained as core_manual.py. This version uses NumPy sorting/reductions for speed; it is not a claim of library-free computation.

Measured results

Important outcome: At 20% noise, averaging wins on farmland and KNN wins on the urban crop. At 5% and 10% in the urban crop, both tested filters increase MSE relative to the noisy input. Small-scale structure is harmed by smoothing. This result must be retained when presenting the experiment.

Interpretation and sources

These 768 × 768 crops preserve source pixels without enlargement. They are from one ESA Sentinel-2 false-colour display composite, not independent satellite acquisitions. Parameter selection cannot eliminate noisy-centre bias or blur. This experiment measures recovery of an 8-bit processed JPEG-derived image from added noise, not recovery of calibrated reflectance or raw sensor noise. RGB processing is channel-wise intensity KNN, not vector-colour or patch-distance KNN.
